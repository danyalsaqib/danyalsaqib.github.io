University Service

I am a part of UBC's Graduate Student Society (GSS), representing the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) as a GSS Councillor. I am a member of the GSS Governance and Accountability and Strategic Planning Committees. We advocate on behalf of graduate students to the university and the broader community.

I am also a member of the ECEGSA, the departmental graduate student body in ECE. I serve as the VP Social in addition to my role as a department councillor.
